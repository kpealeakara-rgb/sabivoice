## "Your BVN or NIN is blocked" calls and messages
A common scam is a call, SMS or WhatsApp message saying your BVN, NIN or account is blocked and asking you to "verify" by sending a code, PIN or clicking a link. Do not respond. Genuine agencies and banks do not ask for your OTP, PIN or password. Check your own numbers yourself with *565*0# (BVN) or *346# (NIN) from your registered phone.
Source: https://sabiguide.com/common-nin-and-bvn-scams/

## Never share your OTP, PIN or password
Your OTP, PIN, password and card CVV are for you alone. Anyone who asks for them, even if they say they are from your bank, NIMC or the government, should be treated as a scammer.
Source: https://sabiguide.com/common-nin-and-bvn-scams/

## If you already shared your OTP or PIN
Contact your bank immediately through the official number on the back of your card or in the bank's app, ask them to block your account or card, and report what happened. Change your PIN and passwords as soon as possible.
Source: https://sabiguide.com/common-nin-and-bvn-scams/

## Links in messages that claim to be from your bank
Do not click links in SMS or WhatsApp messages that claim to be from your bank or a government agency. Type the official website address yourself or use the bank's own app.
Source: https://sabiguide.com/common-nin-and-bvn-scams/
