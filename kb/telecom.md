## Complaining about your network provider
First complain to your network provider (MTN, Airtel, Glo or 9mobile). If they do not resolve it, or you are not satisfied, call the NCC's toll-free line 622 to escalate the complaint so the NCC can step in.
Source: https://www.ncc.gov.ng/media-centre/press-releases/ncc-urges-consumers-take-advantage-622-toll-free-line-and-dnd-2442

## Stopping unwanted promotional SMS and calls
Use the Do-Not-Disturb short code 2442. It lets you block all promotional messages and calls (full DND) or only some categories (partial DND).
Source: https://www.ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2024-11/Documents/research-development/Research-Impact_of_DND_Service_2442_ShortCode.pdf

## Data or services you did not subscribe to
The NCC told network providers to stop automatically renewing data plans without the subscriber's prior consent. If you are charged for a service you did not agree to, complain to your provider and escalate to 622 if it is not fixed.
Source: https://www.telecompaper.com/news/ncc-encourages-use-of-622-toll-free-line-for-complaints-2442-short-code-for-unsolicited-texts--1262378
