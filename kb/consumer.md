## Filing a complaint against a company
If a business sold you a bad product, overcharged you or refused to fix a problem, you can file a complaint with the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC). The FCCPC now uses a web-based complaint portal instead of paper forms. File and track your complaint at user.fccpc.gov.ng.
Source: https://fccpc.gov.ng/consumers/complaint-handling/

## Before you complain to the FCCPC
Complain to the company first and keep evidence: receipts, screenshots, the date and the name of the staff you spoke to. Include these when you file with the FCCPC so your case can be handled quickly.
Source: https://fccpc.gov.ng/consumers/complaint-handling/

## Fake, faulty or substandard products and refunds
If a shop sells you a fake, faulty or substandard product (for example a phone that is not genuine or stops working soon after purchase) and refuses to replace it or refund you, that is a consumer complaint. Keep your receipt and evidence, complain to the seller first, and if they refuse, file a complaint with the FCCPC on its online portal at user.fccpc.gov.ng.
Source: https://fccpc.gov.ng/consumers/complaint-handling/
