## How to find your NIN on your phone
Dial *346# from the phone number you used when you enrolled for your NIN, then choose 1 for "NIN Retrieval". Your 11-digit NIN appears on screen. A small fee (about ₦20) is taken from your airtime. It only works from the SIM linked to your NIN record.
Source: https://www3.nimc.gov.ng/sms-service/

## If you no longer have the phone number linked to your NIN
The *346# code will not work from a different line. Use the NIMC self-service options on nimc.gov.ng or visit a NIMC enrolment centre with your details.
Source: https://explainer.ng/nigeria-id-guides/how-to-check-your-nin-number/

## If you have never enrolled for a NIN
You cannot check a NIN you do not have. Enrol at a NIMC centre or an approved enrolment agent, where your details, fingerprints and photo are captured before a NIN is issued.
Source: https://explainer.ng/nigeria-id-guides/how-to-check-your-nin-number/

## How to check your BVN
Dial *565*0# from the phone number linked to your bank account and your 11-digit BVN shows on screen. A small fee of about ₦20 may be charged. You can also see it in your bank's mobile app or ask at your bank.
Source: https://moniepoint.com/blog/how-to-check-your-bank-verification-number-bvn

## NIN and BVN are different numbers
Your BVN is the Bank Verification Number for the banking system, introduced by the CBN with NIBSS in 2014. Your NIN is your National Identification Number from NIMC. Both have 11 digits and are linked to your fingerprints, but they are separate numbers from separate agencies.
Source: https://moniepoint.com/blog/how-to-check-your-bank-verification-number-bvn

## Correcting details on your BVN
BVN corrections are done through your bank, which sends the request to NIBSS with your supporting documents. Changes to name or date of birth need original documents. Your existing BVN keeps working while the correction is processed.
Source: https://naijasabi.com.ng/how-to-check-bvn-on-phone-nigeria-2026/
