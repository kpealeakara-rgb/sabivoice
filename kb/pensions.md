## What the Retirement Savings Account (RSA) is
Under the Contributory Pension Scheme, your monthly pension contributions go into a Retirement Savings Account (RSA) held with a Pension Fund Administrator (PFA) that you choose. Your RSA is identified by a unique PIN issued by PenCom. You should have only one RSA for life; when you change jobs, give your new employer the same RSA PIN.
Source: https://www.pensionnigeria.com/pension-news/pensionnigeria-frequently-asked-questions/

## How much goes into your pension
Employees contribute at least 8% of their monthly emoluments (basic salary, housing and transport allowance) and employers contribute at least 10%, so at least 18% in total goes into your RSA each month. Your own pension contribution is deducted before your income tax is worked out.
Source: https://www.countrytaxcalc.com/tax-guides/nigeria-income-tax-guide-2026/

## Deadline for employers to remit pension
The Pension Reform Act 2014 says employers must pay pension contributions into their workers' RSAs not later than seven working days after salaries are paid. Not remitting is a breach of the law, not a small admin mistake.
Source: https://www.withinnigeria.com/2026/06/30/how-to-check-your-pension-balance-in-nigeria-through-your-pfa-online-portal/

## Check whether your pension is being paid
Check your RSA statement through your PFA's app, online portal or customer care. If months are missing even though your payslip shows pension deductions, your employer may not be remitting.
Source: https://www.withinnigeria.com/2026/06/30/how-to-check-your-pension-balance-in-nigeria-through-your-pfa-online-portal/

## Reporting an employer that deducts but does not remit
Write a complaint to PenCom with your full name, RSA PIN, your employer's name and address, and the months that are outstanding. Attach payslips showing the deductions and your RSA statement showing the gaps. You can report anonymously. PenCom uses recovery agents to make the employer pay the outstanding contributions plus a penalty into your RSA, at no cost to you.
Source: https://www.thecable.ng/pension-insight-pencom-empowers-employees-to-report-non-compliance/
