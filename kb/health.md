## What the NHIA is
The National Health Insurance Authority (NHIA) was set up under the National Health Insurance Authority Act, signed into law on 19 May 2022. It replaced the old National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS). Its aim is universal health coverage: giving all Nigerians financial access to quality healthcare.
Source: https://www.nhia.gov.ng/3177-2/

## Who your health insurance covers
Under the NHIA scheme for employees, contributions cover the employee, their spouse and up to four biological children.
Source: https://www.nhia.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NHIA-Act-2022-Gazetted-Copy.pdf

## Help for poor and vulnerable Nigerians
The NHIA Act created a Vulnerable Group Fund to pay for health insurance for vulnerable and indigent Nigerians. It is funded from sources including the Basic Health Care Provision Fund, a health insurance levy, government funds, grants and donations.
Source: https://journals.lww.com/npmj/fulltext/2022/29040/the_nigeria_national_health_insurance_authority.1.aspx

## Problems with your hospital or HMO
If your hospital or HMO refuses you a service your plan covers, first complain to your HMO in writing. If it is not resolved, contact the NHIA through the contact details on its official website, nhia.gov.ng, with your enrollee ID and what happened.
Source: https://www.nhia.gov.ng/
